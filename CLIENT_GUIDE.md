# DST LogiPack WhatsApp Bot — Quick Guide

Here is a 1-minute guide to get your WhatsApp Bot connected and controlled:

---

## 1. Connect the Bot (Scan QR Code)
1. Open the dashboard website link: **`http://localhost:3000`** (or your live link).
2. On your mobile phone, open **WhatsApp**.
3. Go to **Settings** ➜ **Linked Devices** ➜ **Link a Device**.
4. Scan the QR code displayed on the website.
5. Once scanned, the status on the website will turn green and show **Connected**.

---

## 2. How the ON / OFF Toggle Works
On the dashboard, you will see a switch labeled **Auto-Reply Chatbot**:
* **ON (Green):** The chatbot is active. It will automatically reply to customer queries, quotes, tracking, and menus.
* **OFF (Gray):** The chatbot is paused. No automated replies will be sent. Use this when your team wants to chat with customers manually.
